How Rainwater Harvesting Works

1

Catchment Area

Rain falls on your rooftop (the catchment surface). Larger area = more water collected.

2

Collection & Filtration

Water flows through gutters and pipes into a first-flush diverter and filter system.

3

Storage

Clean water is stored in tanks or underground sumps for later use.

4

Reuse

Use the stored water for gardening, toilet flushing, cleaning, or groundwater recharge.

The Formula We Use

Harvestable Water (Litres) = Roof Area (m²) × Rainfall (mm) × Runoff Coefficient × Efficiency

Note: 1 mm of rainfall on 1 m² area produces 1 litre of water (theoretical maximum). The runoff coefficient accounts for losses due to absorption, evaporation, and surface type.

Why Harvest Rainwater?

Reduce Water Bills

Less dependency on municipal supply means lower monthly water charges.

Combat Water Scarcity

Help reduce pressure on groundwater and surface water sources.

Improve Groundwater

Recharge aquifers and raise water table in your area over time.

Flood Control

Reduce surface runoff and urban flooding during heavy rains.

Eco-Friendly

Soft, chemical-free water ideal for plants and non-potable uses.

Self-Reliance

Greater water security during shortages or supply disruptions.
